End-of-Pre-Camp Student Diagnostic and Reflection
Form introduction
From Subjects to Systems: Preparing for Jobs, Higher Studies and Entrepreneurship in an AI World
This survey helps us understand your current career outlook, learning habits, intellectual exposure, approach to unfamiliar problems, use of AI and interest in deeper learning opportunities.
Some questions are intended to understand the overall student population. Some questions assess reasoning and learning readiness. These responses may be used to prepare a shortlist of approximately 125–150 students for the two-day Systems Thinking Bootcamp. The college will make the final selection of 100 students after considering academic performance and relevant institutional records.
This is not a university examination. Your responses will not affect your marks, attendance or placement eligibility.
Please answer based on what you genuinely do today—not what you believe is the ideal answer.
